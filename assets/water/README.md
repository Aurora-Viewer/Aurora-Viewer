# Water textures

The foam of enhanced water (whitecaps, the foam they leave, wakes, the
lace at the shore and at the foot of a crash).

| File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `foam-lace-height.png` | "Foam 003" from ambientCG, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foam003 | CC0 1.0 |

Changes: the 2K opacity map (the lace) and displacement map (its
thickness, stretched to the full 8-bit range), downscaled to 1024 × 1024
(Lanczos) and packed into one grey and alpha PNG: grey the lace, alpha the
thickness.

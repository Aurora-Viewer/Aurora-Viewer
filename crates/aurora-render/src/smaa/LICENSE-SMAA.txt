area.bin (160x560 RG8) and search.bin (64x16 R8) are the SMAA precomputed
lookup textures, extracted byte for byte from the Firestorm viewer sources
(indra/newview/SMAAAreaTex.h and SMAASearchTex.h), which carry them from the
original SMAA distribution. shaders/smaa.wgsl is a WGSL port of SMAA.hlsl as
shipped in Firestorm (app_settings/shaders/class1/deferred/SMAA.glsl).
